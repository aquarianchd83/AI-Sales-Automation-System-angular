Setup guide

How to connect (for you) Takes about a minute

Before you start

  • You need the Facebook login that can open your ad account in Meta Ads Manager or Meta Business Suite. Any access level that can see results is enough; we only read.
  • The ads should have been run through that ad account. Ads you paid for through an agency's own ad account won't show up here.
  • You must be an Admin here to connect.

Connect

  1. Click Connect with Facebook above.
  2. Log in to Facebook if asked, then press Continue to allow read-only access to your ad spend.
  3. If your login has several ad accounts, choose the one you run your ads from. With one account this step is skipped.
  4. Wait a few seconds while we pull your last two years of ad spend. After that it updates by itself every day.
  5. Open Reports → Revenue to see social media against WhatsApp.

If something goes wrong

"That Facebook login has no ad account we can read"
You logged in with a Facebook profile that has no access to your ad account. Disconnect, then connect again with the profile you use in Ads Manager.
"Please reconnect Facebook"
Facebook stopped accepting the saved login, usually after a password change or when access was removed. Click Reconnect; your earlier spend is kept.
"This Facebook login has expired or did not start here"
The login link is only valid for 15 minutes and only from the page that started it. Click Connect with Facebook again.
The login screen says the app isn't available, or you can't continue
The platform's Meta App is still waiting for Facebook's approval, so only invited people can connect. Ask your platform administrator to add you as a tester (see the setup below).
You don't run Facebook or Instagram ads
Use Type in your monthly spend instead above. Nothing to connect.
 Meta app setup (platform administrator, one time) Needed before anyone can connect

This is done once for the whole platform, not per tenant. If your platform already has a Meta App for WhatsApp, reuse it: you add the Facebook Login product and a redirect address, and no new App ID or secret is needed. Meta renames menus from time to time, so follow the intent of each step if a label differs.

  1. Open your Meta App. Go to developers.facebook.com/apps and open the app you use for WhatsApp, or choose Create App (type Business) for a new one.
  2. Add the Facebook Login product. In the app dashboard choose Add product and set up Facebook Login (the classic one, not "for Business").
  3. Add the redirect address. Under Facebook Login → Settings, paste this exactly into Valid OAuth Redirect URIs and switch on Client OAuth Login and Web OAuth Login:
    {{ redirectUri }}
    It must match character for character, including https and no trailing slash. Add one entry per address people use (for example a test site as well).
  4. Fill in the basics. Under Settings → Basic add an app icon, a Privacy Policy URL and a category. Facebook requires these before it will approve ad access.
  5. Give the app its ID and secret. Copy App ID and App Secret from Settings → Basic. If the app is the same one WhatsApp uses, nothing to do: its saved ID and secret are reused. Otherwise, in the Platform Admin Console open Configuration, choose the MetaAds category and save MetaAds:AppId and MetaAds:AppSecret. Changes apply immediately, with no restart.
  6. Ask Facebook for ad-spend access. Under App Review → Permissions and Features request ads_read and choose Advanced Access. Facebook will ask you to complete Business Verification, describe how you use the data (read-only ad spend and results, shown to the business that owns the ads), and upload a short screen recording of a tenant connecting and seeing the Revenue report. Approval can take days to weeks.
  7. While you wait: add testers. Until Facebook approves, the app is in Development mode and only people with a role on the app can connect. Add them under App Roles → Roles (as Testers); they accept the invite in Facebook.
  8. Go live. After approval, switch the app from Development to Live (the toggle at the top of the dashboard). From then on any tenant can connect.
