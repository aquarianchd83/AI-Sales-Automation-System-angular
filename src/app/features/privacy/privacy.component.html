{{ productName }} is a sales automation platform that helps businesses ("tenants") talk to their customers on WhatsApp and SMS, manage leads, bookings and campaigns, and create marketing content. This page explains how the app uses, discloses and manages user data.

1. Who is responsible for the data

Each business that uses {{ productName }} decides why and how its customers' data is processed; for that data the business is the controller and {{ productName }} acts as its processor. For the account data of the business's own staff (name, email, sign-in details), {{ productName }} is the controller.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, role, hashed password, and sign-in activity of staff users.
  • Business data: business profile, product and service knowledge, message templates, bookings, packages and settings.
  • Customer and lead data: name, phone number, email, tags, consent status, and conversation history (WhatsApp and SMS messages and media) that the business holds about its customers.
  • Billing data: plan, credit usage and payment records. Card and UPI details are entered with our payment provider and are never stored by us.
  • Connected-account data: when a business connects WhatsApp Business or Meta (Facebook/Instagram) accounts, we receive the identifiers, access tokens and data those platforms permit, such as phone number details, messages, lead-form submissions and ad performance.
  • Usage and technical data: audit-log entries, IP address, browser type, and analytics about how the app is used.

3. How we use data

  • To provide the service: sending and receiving messages, managing leads, bookings, campaigns and reports.
  • To generate content and replies with AI features, only when a business uses them.
  • To process payments, meter credits and prevent abuse or fraud.
  • To secure accounts, keep audit logs, troubleshoot and improve the product.
  • To send service messages such as verification and password-reset emails.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use customers' messages to build advertising profiles.

4. Consent and messaging

Businesses may only message customers who have agreed to be contacted. {{ productName }} provides a consent link that customers receive by SMS, records their choice, and honours opt-outs. A customer can withdraw consent at any time through that link or by telling the business directly.

5. Who we share data with

We share data only with service providers needed to run the product, and only as far as necessary:

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform, Facebook and Instagram) to send and receive messages and to run ads and lead forms.
  • AI providers (for text, image and video generation) which receive the prompts and content a user submits to an AI feature.
  • Payment provider (Razorpay) to process payments.
  • SMS and email delivery providers to deliver messages and system emails.
  • Analytics providers (Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity) to understand product usage.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers that store and process data on our behalf.

We may also disclose data when required by law or to protect rights, safety and the security of the service.

6. Data storage, security and retention

Data is stored in access-controlled databases and transmitted over encrypted connections. Each business's data is isolated from other businesses, access is role-based, and sensitive actions are audit-logged. We keep data for as long as the account is active and as needed to meet legal obligations; after an account is closed, data is deleted or anonymised within a reasonable period.

7. Your rights and data deletion

You may ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent. If your data is held by a business that uses {{ productName }}, please contact that business first; we will assist it in responding. To request deletion of data we hold, email {{ contactEmail }}; deletion is completed within 30 days. See our Data Deletion Instructions for the full process.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

We use browser storage to keep you signed in and remember preferences, and analytics cookies to measure usage. You can block cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work.

9. Children

{{ productName }} is intended for businesses and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect children's data.

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest revision. Material changes will be announced in the app.
