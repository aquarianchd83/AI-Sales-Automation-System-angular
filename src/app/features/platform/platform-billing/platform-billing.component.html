Plan catalog

There are two plans - Trial (the minimum, free, with a one-time starter balance) and Premium (every feature, with credits every month) - and neither expires. Retired plans are kept under Show retired so existing subscribers keep their limits. Each plan is priced per country, in that country's currency; the price shown on a plan is India's, in rupees. A country with no price does not sell the plan. Tax is added on top at checkout.
