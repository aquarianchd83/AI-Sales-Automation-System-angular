Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: {{ lastUpdated }}

This page explains how a business ("tenant") using {{ productName }}, or a person whose data is held in {{ productName }}, can ask for that data to be deleted, what gets deleted, and how long it takes.

1. How to request deletion

Data deletion is handled by email. To request it:

  1. Send an email to {{ contactEmail }} with the subject "Data deletion request".
  2. Send it from the email address registered with your {{ productName }} account, so we can confirm the request is genuine.
  3. Include your business name and registered phone number, and say whether you want the whole account deleted or only specific data (for example, one customer's records).

If the request comes from an address we cannot match to the account, we will reply asking you to verify ownership before we delete anything.

2. How long it takes

Once the request is verified, deletion is completed within 48 hours.

3. What gets deleted

For a full account deletion, we permanently delete:

  • Account data: the workspace, its users (names, emails, sign-in details) and settings.
  • Leads: all leads, including discovered businesses and lead-form submissions.
  • Customers: customer records, phone numbers, emails, tags and consent records.
  • Conversations: WhatsApp and SMS message history and media, campaigns, follow-ups and templates.
  • WhatsApp and Meta connected data: connected WhatsApp Business and Meta (Facebook/Instagram) account identifiers and access tokens, and data received from those platforms. The connection to your WhatsApp Business account is removed from {{ productName }}.

We may keep a limited record of payments and invoices where the law requires it (for example, for tax purposes). This record is not used for any other purpose.

4. Confirmation

When deletion is complete, we send a confirmation email to the address the request came from. Deleted data cannot be recovered, so please export anything you want to keep before sending the request.

5. If you are a customer of a business

If a business contacted you through {{ productName }} and you want your data removed, you can ask that business directly, or email {{ contactEmail }} with your phone number and the business's name. We will work with the business to complete the deletion within the same 48 hours.

6. Contact

Questions about data deletion: {{ contactEmail }}.
